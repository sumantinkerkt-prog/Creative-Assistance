About these tools

Each tool here earned its place by being something you need mid-task, when stopping to find a website would break your concentration.

  • Quick Notes keeps a half-finished thought safe. It even remembers what you were typing if you close the tab by accident.
  • Word Counter matters the moment you're writing to a limit — a caption, a title, a description, a brief.
  • Focus Timer splits work into blocks with a short break between them. It counts how many you finish each day, which is quietly motivating.
  • Colour Palette builds five colours that sit well together, spaced evenly around the colour wheel and stepped from dark to light. Click any colour to copy its code.
  • Checklist is for routines you repeat — publishing steps, packing a kit, a pre-delivery review. Build it once, untick it, run it again.

More tools will be added over time, and none of them will ever ask you to sign up.
