Privacy

The short version: this site has no accounts, no servers and no tracking, so there is nothing of yours for anyone to collect.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login, no contact form and no analytics running on this site. We do not know who you are, and we have no way to find out.

Where your work is kept

Every project, task, note, checklist and saved palette you create is written into your own web browser's local storage on the device you are using. It stays on that device. It is never transmitted to us or to anyone else, because there is no server here to receive it.

Two practical consequences worth understanding:

  • Your work will not appear on your other devices. What you create on a laptop will not show up on a phone.
  • If you clear your browser's data or history for this site, or use a private browsing window, your work will be deleted. We cannot recover it for you, because we never had a copy.

Use the Save a backup button on the Dashboard to download everything as a single file. Keep that file somewhere safe. You can restore it here at any time, on any device.

Cookies

This site sets no cookies. It uses local storage, which is a browser feature for saving data on your own machine. Unlike a cookie, it is never attached to a request or sent over the internet.

Hosting

The site's pages are served as static files by our hosting provider. Like virtually all web hosts, they may keep basic server logs of requests, which can include an IP address, for security and reliability purposes. We do not have access to those logs and do not use them.

Third parties and advertising

At present this site loads no third-party scripts, no fonts from external services, no embedded content and no advertising.

If advertising is introduced in future, this page will be updated beforehand to name the advertising provider and explain what it does. Any such provider may use cookies or similar technologies to select and measure ads, and you would be able to manage that through your browser settings and, where applicable, the provider's own controls. Even then, the content you create in the app would remain on your device and would not be shared with advertisers.

Children

This site is a general-purpose set of productivity tools. It does not knowingly gather information from anyone, including children, because it gathers no information at all.

Changes to this page

If how the site handles information ever changes, this page will be updated to describe the new position accurately.

Contact

Questions about this site can be raised through the repository it is published from.
